# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React, Tailwind CSS v4, react-router-dom

## Users

Personal brands and creators looking for unique visual identities, illustrations, and web experiences.

## Product Purpose

A creative studio portfolio and internal dashboard. Blends hand-drawn illustration by Aline with cutting-edge web development and AI technology by Ryan.

## Positioning

Combining authentic, hand-drawn art with modern technology. Every artwork slot must be genuine and hand-drawn (no AI-generated illustrations).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All illustration/artwork is hand-drawn by Aline. No AI-generated imagery for artworks.
- Contact form uses Formspree with a hidden honeypot field.
- Images should use placeholders if actual hand-drawn art is not yet available.

## Brand Commitments

- **Aesthetic**: Frutiger Aero.
- **Theme**: Light mode (White background, black primary text).
- **Style**: Glossy glassmorphism (glass-panel, bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-3xl), floating elements, rounded corners (3rem).
- **Colors**: Bright blues and emerald greens (cyan/green gradients).
- **Tone**: Professional, creative, cutting-edge yet authentic.
